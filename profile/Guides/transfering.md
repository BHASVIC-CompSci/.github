## Transfering repositories on Github

### Why do you need to transfer a repository?

All repositories in the BHASVIC organisation made by old students will be deleted after the student leaves BHASVIC 
so to ensure that no student loses data they want to keep we have them transfer their repository to a personal account before they leave.

E.g. 
Transfering a repository from BHASVIC-JohnDoe26 to just JohnDoe2020 (a personal account)


### How to transfer a repository

#### BHASVIC Side
1. On GitHub, navigate to the main page of the repository.
2. Under your repository name, click Settings.
3. Under "Danger zone", click transfer the repository.
4. Click specify organization or username.
5. Type in your personal accounts username
6. Click on the confirmation text box and type the repository name.
7. Click Transfer

#### Personal Side
1. Open your email inbox for the email used to create your personal github.
2. Click on the lastest emails from github
3. Click the accept link to accept the repository transfer.
4. Verify the repostiory has be transfered onto your personal account.
